# Preset names mapping

For completeness we report the mapping between the Adobe Lightroom (LR) presets abbreviations reported in the plots and their full names as displayed in the LR interface.
Preset names follow a three-level hierarchy: [Category] -> [Preset Family] -> [Preset Name] (i.e., Adaptive -> Blur Background -> Strong).

We recall the presets categories defined in the paper:

- Adaptive (AD)
- Style (ST)
- Seasons(SS)
- Subject (SJ)
